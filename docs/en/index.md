## Module List

### Financial Management

- [Recurring Income](./recurring-income.md) - Manage regular income sources (salary, rent, etc.)

- [Recurring Expenses](./recurring-expense.md) - Manage recurring expenses (utilities, etc.)
- [Daily Expenses](./daily-expense.md) - Record daily expenses
- [Budget](./budget.md) - Plan and track monthly budgets
- [Savings](./savings.md) - Manage savings accounts
- [Bank Loans](./bank-debt.md) - Manage loans and debt payments
- [Financial Goals](./financial-goals.md) - Set and track financial goals

- [Special Occasions](./special-occasions.md) - Manage special occasions and preparation checklists
- [Todo List](./todo-list.md) - Manage recurring tasks and track completion progress

- [Reports](./reports.md) - View detailed financial reports

- [Settings](./settings.md) - Configure app, categories, language, currency
- [Backup & Restore](./backup-restore.md) - Backup and restore data
