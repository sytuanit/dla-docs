# Request to Delete Your Data — Daily Life Assistant

**Daily Life Assistant** (developer: Havasoft) lets you delete app data **without deleting your cloud account** (email sign-in). Follow the steps below.

**Last updated:** May 2026

---

## Option 1 — Delete specific data (in the app)

Use this when you only want to remove some entries.

1. Open **Daily Life Assistant** on your device.
2. Go to the screen for the item you want to remove (expense, income, task, recipe, goal, etc.).
3. Use **Delete** / swipe-to-delete (as shown on that screen).
4. Confirm deletion.

**What is deleted:** only the item(s) you choose.

**What is kept:** your account (if signed in), all other data, Premium status on the device.

**Retention:** deleted items are removed from the app immediately. If you use cloud sync, the change is synced to our cloud backend (Supabase) when online.

---

## Option 2 — Delete all app data but keep your account

Use this to erase everything in the app and start fresh, **without** deleting your email login.

1. Open **Daily Life Assistant**.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Open **Backup & data** (or the backup/data section).
4. Tap **Clear app data** / **Delete all data**.
5. Read the confirmation message and confirm.

**What is deleted:**

- All ledgers and details on this device (income, expenses, budgets, savings, loans, goals, todos, recipes, menus, shopping lists, etc.)
- If you are signed in to a cloud account and use cloud sync: the matching copy stored for your account on our cloud backend

**What is kept:**

- Your cloud **account** (email sign-in) — you stay signed in unless you sign out
- **Premium** status stored on this device (if any)
- Payment records held by **Google Play** / **App Store** (not in the app)

**Retention:** deletion is immediate in the app; cloud mirror is cleared when the wipe completes successfully. We do not keep a separate copy of wiped ledger data for recovery.

**Note:** If you are a **member** of someone else’s shared book, this action may be restricted until you leave that book.

---

## Option 3 — Delete local data only (uninstall)

1. Uninstall **Daily Life Assistant** from your device.

**What is deleted:** all data stored only on that device.

**What is kept:** data on our cloud backend if you had cloud sync enabled and were signed in (until you use Option 2 while signed in, or Option 4).

---

We aim to respond within **30 days**. Account deletion on our backend is completed after we verify the request; we do not retain deleted account credentials beyond what is required for legal or security obligations.

## Data we do not control

- **Backup files** you exported to Google Drive, iCloud, or other locations — delete those files where you saved them.
- **Google Play** purchase and subscription history — manage via your Google account.
